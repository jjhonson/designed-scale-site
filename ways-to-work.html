Ways to work together

Two ways to work together.

Designed Scale works with companies in two ways: taking on defined commercial challenges with agreed milestones, or providing ongoing executive leadership. The right approach depends on the problem, the company's existing capabilities, and the level of involvement required.

Milestone-based commercial engagements

Agreed work. Defined milestones. Not billable hours.

What the work can include
  • Evaluating markets, customer segments and commercial opportunities
  • Testing positioning, pricing, packaging or business models
  • Developing an enterprise go-to-market strategy
  • Designing a repeatable commercial process and operating model
  • Preparing a new product or technology for commercialization
How it works

Each engagement starts by agreeing on what needs to be accomplished, the decisions to be made and the milestones that mark progress. The work is scoped and priced around those milestones, rather than a commitment of hours or days.

Milestones represent work completed, evidence developed, decisions made or capabilities established, not guarantees of revenue, financing or results outside the company's control.

An engagement may run for several months, with each phase building on what is learned and the next set of milestones agreed together.

Fractional commercial leadership

Ongoing executive responsibility without a full-time hire.

What it can include
  • Fractional CRO, CCO or CMO leadership
  • Growth strategy, sales and marketing leadership
  • Organizational development and operating processes
  • Forecasting and day-to-day commercial decisions
  • Leadership through a transition or toward a permanent executive hire
How it works

Some companies need more than help with a defined challenge. They need an experienced executive to take responsibility for commercial strategy, organization and execution over time.

Designed Scale works directly with the CEO and the existing team. Unlike a milestone-based engagement, fractional leadership involves ongoing executive ownership. Responsibilities, availability and working arrangements are agreed around what the business needs.
