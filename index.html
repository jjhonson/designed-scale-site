Where I help

Common situations.

I usually get involved when a growth problem crosses sales, marketing, product and strategy. The company has something real, but the way it goes to market is no longer keeping pace with the business.

01

Traction without a repeatable sales motion.

Customers get value, but acquisition is still bespoke, founder-dependent or difficult to predict. I help identify enough common structure in the sale for the company to learn, manage and scale.

02

The move upmarket.

Early adoption can look very different from enterprise buying. Positioning, packaging, proof, process and organization often have to change together.

03

An operating model built for an earlier stage.

Roles, stages, handoffs, forecasting and tools evolved organically. The CRM often reflects the underlying process problems.

04

Too many products, markets or directions.

The company is active across too many opportunities, and priorities are no longer clear enough to guide resources and execution.

05

A new stage for the business.

A funding round, new product, acquisition, geography or leadership change can expose limits in the existing GTM model. I help redesign it around the new stage of the business.

Where I work

Where this work tends to fit.

The mandate changes with the scale of the company. In both settings, I am usually brought in around a meaningful strategic or commercial transition.

Growth-stage technology companies

Companies with real product and customer evidence whose commercial model has not matured at the same pace. I may take operating ownership, run a focused project, or work fractionally with the CEO and team.

Typical situations: founder-led sales → repeatable team motion · developer adoption → enterprise · bespoke success → scalable offer · post-funding GTM buildout

Larger companies and business units

In larger companies, the work is more often a consequential, bounded problem: growth strategy, market entry, GTM redesign, positioning and packaging, or a new operating model for a business entering its next stage.

Typical situations: new product or market · business-unit reset · portfolio strategy · commercial-model redesign · executive strategy work
